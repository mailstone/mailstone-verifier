About MailStone Verifier

What is MailStone Verifier?

MailStone Verifier is a standalone application that allows you to independently verify blockchain-anchored email proofs generated by the MailStone system.

Features

  • Hash Calculation: Calculate SHA-256 cryptographic hashes of files (PDFs, attachments, JSON)
  • TimeStamp Decoder: Decode and verify RFC 3161 timestamp tokens from trusted TSA providers
  • Merkle Verification: Verify the inclusion of a hash in a Merkle tree and reconstruct the root

Privacy & Security

All verification is performed locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers.
