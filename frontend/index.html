Recipient Decision Signature (Ed25519)

The recipient signed their decision (accept / refuse) with a private key that never left their device. Give the verifier the three elements printed in the proof document — the signed text, the public key and the signature — and it answers true or false. Nothing is recomputed or compared by hand.

Where to find it in the Dossier de preuve — section « Décision du destinataire » : Clé publique du destinataire (Ed25519, base64), Signature Ed25519 (base64), and the four-line block under Message exact signé par le destinataire. Line breaks that the page inserted inside the key or the signature are ignored.

Event Evidence Hash

Each event of a delivery is anchored as one leaf of a Merkle tree. The leaf is a SHA-256 — of the Email PDF for the content, of a short canonical text listing the event's facts for every other event. Three links, each checkable here: facts → leaf (this card rebuilds the leaf from the facts), leaf → root (the event's proof block lists the sibling hashes that lead from the leaf to the Racine Merkle), root → date and immutability (the TSA token of the same card covers that root, and the Stellar transaction carries it).

Where to find it in the Dossier de preuve — the facts are in the « Envoi » table (expéditeur, destinataire, objet, Empreinte du contenu) and in the « Chronologie » / « Notification au destinataire » sections (dates, Identifiant de message Postmark). Each « Preuve d'étape — … » card holds the event's Racine Merkle, its Jeton ASN.1 horodaté and its Preuve Merkle (JSON); in that JSON, leaf_hash is the anchored leaf and root_hash the root. Line breaks the page inserted inside a hash or an identifier are ignored.

About MailStone Verifier

What is MailStone Verifier?

MailStone Verifier is a standalone application that allows you to independently verify blockchain-anchored email proofs generated by the MailStone system.

Features

  • Hash Calculation: Calculate SHA-256 cryptographic hashes of files (PDFs, attachments, JSON)
  • TimeStamp Decoder: Decode and verify RFC 3161 timestamp tokens from trusted TSA providers
  • Merkle Verification: Verify the inclusion of a hash in a Merkle tree and reconstruct the root
  • ERE Verification: Check the recipient's Ed25519 decision signature and recompute the evidence hash of each event of a Registered Electronic Delivery

Privacy & Security

All verification is performed locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers.
